What's in the CA workspace

Same across every tier — tiers differ by firm size and seats, not by feature gating.

Readiness score

Per client, built from intake answers: what's logged, what's still missing — Form 130, AIS, rent receipts + landlord PAN, Section 126 certificates, home-loan certificate, NPS statement.

Bulk requests

"All clients missing X" generates a pre-filled message, so your team isn't chasing one person at a time.

Automated first-pass checks

Dates outside the FY, cash-payment limits on Section 126/133, missing landlord PAN, likely duplicates — flagged before your team reviews, not after.

Export

Per-client deduction schedule (Excel + PDF) in the e-filing portal's field order, plus the receipts register, aimed at your existing filing workflow.

What it isn't

  • Not an e-filer. No ERI registration, no portal submission — your firm remains the one that reviews and files.
  • Not tax advice. Every figure shown to a client is labelled an estimate.
  • Not a fee-share or referral arrangement. TaxPlot never takes a percentage of your fees or a per-client commission, and never sells firms as leads. It's a tool, priced as a tool.
  • Not a replacement for your practice-management software. It's the layer before it, so cleaner data reaches it.

Questions firms ask us

When can my firm actually start?

The CA-workspace backend (the consent flow that lets a client securely share receipts with your firm) is in active development and isn't live yet. Joining the interest list below means we'll contact you the moment a working pilot exists — not before.

How is client data protected, and who can see it?

A client explicitly consents before anything is shared — naming your firm, the purpose, and the financial year. Your firm never holds a separate copy of a client's data: access is granted directly on the client's own record while consent is live, and disappears immediately if they withdraw it, enforced at the database level, not by application logic. The client can see exactly who's accessed their data and when.

Do you take a cut of my fees, or sell client leads?

No. ICAI's own rules (First Schedule Part I) rule out fee-sharing, per-client commissions, and lead-selling to CAs — and we wouldn't want to build that model regardless. Pricing is a flat subscription by firm size, full stop.

Can I cancel, and what happens to my data if I do?

Nothing locks you in — a flat subscription, cancel when you like. Because your firm never held a separate copy of client data in the first place, there's nothing left over to delete on your side either.

Does this replace Winman, Saral, CompuTax, or Gen?

No — TaxPlot sits before those tools, not instead of them. It's where a client's year-round receipts become a ready-to-review queue; your existing return-prep software still does the filing.
